The Senior Manager Growth is a hands-on execution leader and the second-in-command (2IC) to the Head of Growth. This case study is designed to see how you **execute**: how you read data quickly, turn a strategy someone else designed into weekly action, and keep a deal moving when it stalls. We are not testing polish. We are testing judgment, follow-through, and clarity.

- **Coaching Sessions:** Teachers record classroom lessons and receive AI feedback

- **Lesson Plan Generation:** AI-assisted lesson planning
- **Presentation Generation:** AI-assisted teaching presentations
- **General Chat:** Free-form Q&A about teaching and pedagogy

## Assignment 2: Growth Loop Execution Plan

Growth leadership has designed the following loop for Alpha Platform, and it is now yours to run:

> A teacher uses Alpha Platform's coaching feature → receives feedback that visibly improves a lesson → the school administrator notices and asks about it → the administrator invites Taleemabad to run a session for the whole school → more teachers onboard → some of those teachers transfer schools or share in district WhatsApp groups → new schools ask in.

Your job is **not** to redesign the loop. Your job is to make it spin.

**Build a 60-day execution plan that covers:**

- The specific actions you would run at each step of the loop (who does what, through which channel — school visits, WhatsApp, events, direct outreach).
- How you engage the different stakeholder layers (teachers, administrators, district officials) so the loop does not stall at any single layer.
- The metrics you track weekly, including how you would measure this loop's contribution toward a **K-factor of 0.2**.
- How you keep the pipeline visible: what you log, where, and what your weekly update to the Head of Growth contains.
- The two most likely points where the loop breaks, and your contingency for each.

**Submit:** Max 2 pages + one simple tracker (spreadsheet) you would actually use to run this.

## Assignment 3: The Stalled Deal

**Scenario:** Three months ago, Taleemabad ran a successful pilot with a district education authority: 40 schools, strong teacher usage, and the District Education Officer (DEO) publicly praised the results. The DEO verbally committed to expanding to 200 schools.

Since then: six weeks of silence. Procurement says the file is "in process." The DEO has stopped responding to calls. The provincial budget cycle closes in five weeks — if the expansion is not in this budget, it waits a year. Meanwhile, your Head of Growth is asking for a pipeline update, and a competitor has started meeting schools in the same district.

**Your task — produce three things:**

1. **Action plan (1 page):** Your moves for the next five weeks, in sequence. Who you contact, in what order, and what you ask each person for. Be specific about how you re-open a relationship that has gone quiet without damaging it.
2. **The stakeholder email:** The actual email you would send to the DEO this week. (Write it in full — we want to see your judgment on tone, length, and ask.)
3. **Internal update (half page):** What you tell the Head of Growth — including your honest read on the probability this closes, and what help you need.

**Submit:** One document containing all three parts.
