# Persona Brief — Fundraising & Partnerships (2-4 years)

**Role:** Fundraising & Partnerships, Taleemabad
**Band set by Ayesha:** 2-4 years total professional experience (hard cap)
**Location:** Islamabad-first; Pakistan-wide acceptable
**Date:** 2026-09-08

---

## Why the band matters to the search

At 2-4 years nobody has run a bilateral relationship end to end. We are not looking for a
track record of closed grants. We are looking for someone who has been **in the room where
institutional money is raised**, and has owned a real piece of it. The competency framework
is the bar we will assess against at interview, not a checklist the CV must already satisfy.

## Observable markers (what a CV can actually show at 2-4 years)

| Competency (Ayesha's framework) | What we can see at 2-4 years |
|---|---|
| Understanding our organisation | Sector-adjacent: education, development, or social impact. Not a cold-start on the sector. |
| Donor research | Has built a donor map, funder longlist, or opportunity tracker. |
| Donor qualification | Has screened RFPs or calls for proposals and recommended go / no-go. |
| Donor engagement and listening | Has attended donor meetings and owned the follow-up note or action. |
| Storytelling and pitching | Has drafted a concept note, pitch deck, or case for support. |
| Proposal and grant development | **Strongest single signal.** Has written or substantially contributed to a submitted proposal against a donor's own template. |
| Evidence and M&E literacy | Has written donor reports against a logframe or results framework. |
| Cost-effectiveness and scale | Has touched a budget narrative or a cost-per-beneficiary figure. |
| Pipeline management | Has maintained a CRM, grants tracker, or funding pipeline. |

## Where this person currently sits

1. **Education nonprofits** — TCF, ITA, Teach For Pakistan, Zindagi Trust, Teach The World, DIL, PAGE.
2. **Social sector with a real fundraising function** — Akhuwat, NOWPDP, KDSP, Indus Hospital, SOS, AKF, Hashoo.
3. **Donor-funded implementers** — DAI, Chemonics, Palladium, Mott MacDonald, OPM, Cowater, Save the Children, IRC, Plan. This is where junior proposal and bid writers live.
4. **Multilaterals and bilaterals, junior grades** — UNICEF, UNDP, UNESCO, WFP, World Bank, ADB, FCDO, GIZ, EU Delegation, embassies. Islamabad-heavy by definition.
5. **Corporate CSR and Pakistani foundations** — Engro, Dawood, Fatima, Jazz, HBL, Nestlé CSV. They sit on the *other* side of the table and know how the money is released.
6. **Impact capital, think tanks, edtech with B2G revenue** — Karandaaz, i2i, Acumen, Tabadlab, SDPI, CERP, Knowledge Platform, Maqsad.

## Anti-signals (exclude, or tier down hard)

- Retail or individual giving only: zakat drives, street collection, donation boxes, no institutional donor work.
- Pure event management or pure social media and comms.
- Pure programme delivery, teaching, or field coordination with no donor-facing remit.
- Pure M&E with no donor reporting.
- Corporate key account management or channel sales. Rejected as a persona on the Senior Manager Growth
  search for the same reason: selling to a buyer is not raising from a funder.
- Anyone whose title contains Director, Head, VP, Chief, President, Founder, Country Director,
  General Manager, Chief of Party, Partner, or Board Member. Out of band by definition.
