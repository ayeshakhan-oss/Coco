Two corrections from Ayesha on the Job-42 SMG case-study work, 2026-08-17.

## 1. A benchmark answer key contains NO candidate material

> "benchmark doesn't need candidate's response"

I had appended an addendum to
`docs/case_studies/benchmarks/smg_execution_sprint_benchmark.md` listing findings candidates
produced that the answer key missed, attributed by name. She cut it.

**Why:** the benchmark's job is to define the bar *independently of any cohort*. The moment it
carries this round's discoveries, the next reader calibrates against those candidates instead of
against the case — and the key stops being reusable for the GM case study or a later SMG round.

**How to apply:** benchmark = model answer + ground-truth figures + excellence markers + traps,
and nothing else. If cohort findings are worth preserving, they go in a **separate v2 benchmark**
document, still written without candidate names.

## 2. Evaluation reports carry assessment, not internal process

She cut the whole **Pool Verdict** block from the evaluation report. It opened with "stronger
than it looked before the three email-only submissions were read" and referenced "things our own
answer key missed."

**Why:** the report is a decision document that may be forwarded to a hiring panel. Narrative
about our retrieval problems, our answer key's gaps, and how the ranking changed is internal
process — it belongs in chat, not in the artefact.

**How to apply:** report = stat boxes → ranked table (every name hyperlinked) → per-candidate
detail (stands out / thin / proceed / probes) → one-line method note. Keep candour about
candidates; drop candour about our own workflow. The same test applies to the "Method & limits"
section — state what it was scored against and any limit that affects a score, not the story of
how the files were obtained.
