# Values Feedback Email Tone — LOCKED

**Refined through Syeda Siddiqa Fatima (CPD Coach) session.**

## Core Philosophy

At Taleemabad, feedback emails to candidates who didn't clear values must feel **deeply human**. We communicate empathy, care, warmth, and emotional intelligence clearly. Even when we say no, candidates should feel respected, warm, and thoughtful about the experience.

**The purpose is not evaluation — it's care.**

---

## What TO Do

✅ **Warm & Observational**
- "We felt the genuineness in that story"
- "We were hoping to better understand..."
- Gentle observation, not analysis

✅ **Specific Interview Evidence**
- Every observation tied directly to what they actually said
- Use their exact moments: "When you told us about stepping in to cover training schedules..."
- Concrete examples, not generalizations

✅ **Conversational Tone**
- "We" voice always
- Sounds like humans talking, not corporate evaluation
- Natural pacing, short sentences mixed with longer ones

✅ **Empathetic Gaps**
- Frame improvements as "where we'd have loved to understand more"
- Not "where you fell short"
- Not "what you need to fix"

✅ **Actionable but Gentle**
- "As you move forward, we'd encourage you to reflect on experiences..."
- Guidance without prescription
- Not telling them what to do, suggesting what might help

✅ **Affirm in P.S.**
- Brief, warm reaffirmation of what we saw in them
- Belief in their potential
- "We believe in what you're becoming"

---

## What NOT to Do

❌ **Life-Coach Language**
- ~~"Those insights are where real growth lives"~~ → Too philosophical
- ~~"That self-knowledge is something people can feel and respond to"~~ → Too analytical
- ~~"It's magnetic without being forced"~~ → Poetic but creates emotional intensity
- Remove ALL statements that sound like psychological analysis or life guidance

❌ **Internal Jargon**
- ~~"plus-minus ratings"~~
- ~~"mixed signals limits"~~
- ~~"GWC terminology"~~
- ~~"values mechanics"~~
- **NEVER surface internal frameworks directly to candidates**

❌ **Accusatory Language**
- ~~"It felt like a tactic, something you've learned to say"~~ → Questions authenticity, feels personal
- ~~"not something you think you should care about"~~ → Implies performance, inauthenticity
- Remove anything that implies judgment of their character or motives

❌ **Emotional Intensity / Multiple Questions**
- ~~"What made you stay?" "What did you learn about yourself?" "What made you want to quit?"~~ → Overwhelming, introspective
- Avoid clusters of reflective questions
- Don't make them overthink themselves

❌ **Prescriptive Advice**
- ~~"Build a few stories of real challenge and real growth — not to perform them for anyone, but for yourself"~~ → Too introspective, emotionally heavy
- ~~"Know what actually pushed you and changed you"~~ → Sounds like therapy homework

❌ **Shame or Discouragement**
- Never leave them feeling ashamed, hurt, demotivated
- Never make them doubt their value
- Never frame rejection as personal failure

## Tone Markers (Examples)

### ✅ RIGHT
"We were hoping to better understand how certain experiences shaped you personally and professionally over time."
- Gentle, curious, observational
- Not demanding, not analyzing

### ❌ WRONG
"Those moments — when we're in the thick of it — are often where we grow the most."
- Sounds like a life coach
- Too philosophical

---

## Structure (LOCKED)

**Opening:** Gratitude + warm context

**Section 1: What Stood Out**
- 2-3 specific strengths with direct interview evidence
- Use their exact words/stories
- Affirm what you felt/observed

**Section 2: Where We'd Love to Understand More**
- 2-3 gaps, framed as curiosity not critique
- "We were hoping to..." not "You didn't..."
- Specific moments, not generalizations

**Section 3: What We Hope for You**
- Light guidance (reflect, notice, keep growing)
- Not prescriptive, not life advice
- Short, warm, actionable

**P.S. Box:** Reaffirmation of what we saw, belief in their potential

---

## Word Count & Format

- **800-1100 words minimum** — NO exceptions
- **Georgia serif, 11px, justified**
- **Blue headings (#1565C0)**
- **No em dashes** — use period, comma, colon instead
- **"We" voice always**
- **One complete email** — NO Gmail "..." truncation
- **No internal jargon or frameworks**
