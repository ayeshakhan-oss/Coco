## Position Overview

Support Regional Managers in designing and implementing a comprehensive program for government teachers. Conduct needs assessments, facilitate training sessions, and provide individualized coaching to enhance teachers' skills.

**Key Requirement:** Willingness to travel in assigned region at 6–7 am, 100% of the time.

---

## Core Responsibilities

- Support Regional Managers to design and implement professional development program
- Conduct daily school visits and classroom observations
- Identify areas of improvement and facilitate training sessions
- Provide individualized coaching and mentoring to teachers
- Monitor progress and provide constructive feedback
- Collaborate with stakeholders (principals, education officials)
- Stay updated on latest trends in education and digital learning
- Document and maintain training records
- Evaluate program impact through data collection and analysis
- Contribute to development of instructional materials
- Follow organizational guidelines for target achievement
- Support additional assignments from Regional Managers

---

## Required Skills

- Detail-oriented, tech-savvy
- Analyzing problems to come up with resolutions
- Caring attitude toward teachers
- Strong follow-through discipline
- Problem-solving mindset
- Excellent communication skills
- Positive relationship-building aptitude
- Teamwork orientation
- Early travel willingness (6–7 am)

---

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's in Education, Public Policy, Social Sciences, or related field
- Two years relevant experience (or fresh graduates encouraged)
- Previous teaching in primary/secondary schools (plus)
- Training experience (plus)
- Sound knowledge of educational theories, instructional strategies, digital solutions, classroom observation frameworks
- Familiarity with Pakistani education system challenges
- Excellent interpersonal and communication skills
- Strong organizational and time management abilities
- Proficiency in digital tools/platforms
- Fluency in English and Urdu (spoken and written)
