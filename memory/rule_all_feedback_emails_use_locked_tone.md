# RULE — All Candidate Feedback Emails Use Locked Tone

**Effective Date:** 2026-05-12  
**Status:** 🔒 LOCKED & NON-NEGOTIABLE

---

## The Rule

**ALL candidate feedback emails — values feedback, warm bench rejections, GWC rejections, screening rejections, any rejection/feedback — MUST follow the tone guide in:**

**[memory/values_feedback_email_tone_locked_2026_05_12.md](values_feedback_email_tone_locked_2026_05_12.md)**

---

## Applies To

✅ Values feedback emails (candidates who didn't clear values)  
✅ Warm bench rejections (passed values, didn't get selected)  
✅ GWC rejections (failed case study/GWC stage)  
✅ Screening rejections (didn't advance from CV screening)  
✅ Any other candidate rejection or feedback email

---

## Non-Negotiable Requirements

1. **Warm, observational tone** — NOT analytical, NOT life-coach-ish
2. **No internal jargon** — No plus-minus ratings, GWC terminology, values mechanics
3. **No accusatory language** — Never imply inauthenticity or character judgment
4. **Specific interview evidence** — Every observation tied to what they actually said
5. **800-1100 words minimum** — Count before sending
6. **Georgia serif, 11px, justified** — Format locked
7. **Self-QA checklist** — Run all 8 items before submitting to Ayesha
8. **Pilot to Ayesha + Jawad only** — Never candidate directly

## Why This Rule Exists

Session 2026-05-12 refined the tone through multiple iterations with Syeda Siddiqa Fatima (CPD Coach). Key learnings:

- **Life-coach language hurts.** Phrases like "growth lives" and "self-knowledge" create emotional intensity that makes candidates feel over-analyzed
- **Jargon is invisible disrespect.** Internal frameworks should never be surfaced to candidates
- **Specificity builds trust.** Tying feedback to exact moments they said proves we listened
- **Care is the real message.** Even in rejection, candidates should feel respected and warm

This tone protects candidate experience while maintaining honest feedback.

---

## Locked Tone Markers (Quick Reference)

| DO ✅ | DON'T ❌ |
|-------|----------|
| "We were hoping to understand..." | "You didn't..." |
| "We felt the genuineness in that story" | "It felt like a tactic" |
| Specific moments: "When you told us about stepping in..." | Generalizations: "You showed initiative" |
| Light guidance: "As you move forward, we'd encourage you to reflect..." | Prescriptive: "Build a few stories of real growth..." |
| Warm observation | Psychological analysis |
| "We believe in what you're becoming" | "Here's what you need to fix" |
