NIETE = National Institute of Excellence in Teacher Education.

A Taleemabad initiative launched in collaboration with the Ministry of Federal Education and Professional Training (MoFEPT). It is a digital teacher training and licensing programme operating as a 360-degree ecosystem for teachers, school leaders, students, and parents.

**Key facts:**
- Website: niete.edu.pk
- Run by Taleemabad in partnership with MoFEPT
- Focuses on CPD (Continuous Professional Development) for teachers
- AI-powered tools for assessments, lesson plans, coaching
- Digital training in partnership with LUMS School of Education
- Currently operating in Islamabad (urban and suburban)
- Hiring manager for Job 17 (CPD Coach): Hasnat Tariq — Hasnat@niete.edu.pk

**Why:** When working on NIETE-related jobs (e.g. Job 17 CPD Coach), understand this is a Taleemabad sister project, not a third party. Treat NIETE roles with the same mission-alignment lens as Taleemabad roles.
