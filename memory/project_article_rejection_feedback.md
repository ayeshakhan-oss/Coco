# Article: "Most rejection emails say nothing. We decided to change that."

Published / drafted: 2026-04-01
Authors credited: Coco, Noah, Nugget (AI assistants), P&C team
Special thanks: Jawwad Ali, Ayesha Raza Khan, Aymen Abid

## What the article is about
How Taleemabad built an agentic pipeline to send personalized rejection feedback at scale.
- 160+ candidates received individual feedback emails for a Field Coordinator (R&I) role
- Process went from 8 hours manual work for 5-10 emails to 1 hour for 160+
- Article covers: intent, the manual first attempt, what was built, the fear before sending, the responses, learnings, and what comes next

## Core themes
- Teach well and improve applied to hiring
- Scale and warmth are not opposites
- The rejection email is the truest version of your culture
- The way you say no tells people more about you than the way you say yes

## Writing rules confirmed during this session
- No dashes anywhere (existing SOP confirmed applies to articles too)
- "Teach well and improve" should never be reduced to a slogan — frame as a commitment
- Never use the word "students" in external-facing P&C content — use "people" or "learners" instead
- Article tone: honest, slightly vulnerable, not corporate, not LinkedIn-speak
- Bullet lists weaken the most important sections — convert to short prose
