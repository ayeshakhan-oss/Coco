# Warm Bench Email Subject Lines - LOCKED PATTERN

**RULE: POETIC & STORY-BASED ONLY**

Subject lines are NOT position + candidate name. They are STORIES tied to a specific interview moment.

---

## Examples

✅ **CORRECT (Poetic, story-based):**
- "The Principal's Expressions Changed When Data Spoke" (Hajra Sajjad, CPD Coach - 2026-05-15)
- "When Gestures Speak Louder" 
- "The Journal That Proved Your Resilience"
- "The Moment You Chose the Team Over the Title"
- "Data, Trust, and a Changed Mind"

❌ **WRONG (Generic, evaluative):**
- "Hajra Sajjad - CPD Coach Position Update"
- "Position Update - Warm Bench"
- "Interview Feedback"
- "Your Application Status"

---

## How to Write Them

1. **Find the keystone moment** - What's the ONE story from their interview that defines them?
2. **Tell it as a headline** - Poetic, emotional, NOT evaluative
3. **Tie to emotional truth** - The moment that stayed with the panel
4. **Never use:** "Interview," "feedback," role names, candidate names, "warm bench" terminology

---

## Pattern Structure

**[MOMENT] + [ACTION/REALIZATION] + [CONSEQUENCE]**

Examples:
- "The Principal's Expressions Changed" (moment) "When Data Spoke" (consequence)
- "When Gestures Speak Louder" (action reveals consequence)
- "The Journal That Proved Your Resilience" (object + revelation)

---

## Integration with Email

Subject line should CONNECT to opening paragraph.

**Example:**
- **Subject:** "The Principal's Expressions Changed When Data Spoke"
- **Opening:** "You described standing in front of a principal who had said no..." [tells the story from the subject]

---

## Why This Matters

1. **Emotional resonance** - Candidate opens email with recognition, not dread
2. **Dignity preservation** - Not labeled as "rejection" or "feedback"
3. **Memorable** - They'll remember and repeat this email
4. **Sets tone** - Before reading body, emotional message is clear: "we see you, we remember you"

---

**This pattern is LOCKED IN.** Every warm bench email subject must follow this approach.
