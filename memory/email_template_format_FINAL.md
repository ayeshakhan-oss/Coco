# Email Template Format - FINAL EXACT SPECIFICATIONS

**THIS IS THE PRECISE FORMAT FOR ALL REJECTION AND FEEDBACK EMAILS.**

## Structure (Top to Bottom):

1. **Logo** (centered, ~0.8 inch height)
   - Taleemabad logo image at very top

2. **Small Header Text** (centered, blue #1565c0, uppercase, letter-spaced)
   - "PEOPLE &amp; CULTURE • [TYPE]"
   - Examples: "PEOPLE &amp; CULTURE • REJECTION DECISION", "PEOPLE &amp; CULTURE • APPLICATION UPDATE"
   - Font size: 10-11px

3. **Large Blue Position Title** (centered, BOLD, blue #1565c0, prominent)
   - Main role/position name
   - Examples: "Your Application for Junior Research Associate", "Hackathon 2026 Position"
   - Font size: 16-18px, bold

4. **Smaller Blue Position Subtitle** (centered, blue #1565c0)
   - Department/function/context
   - Examples: "Junior Research Associate, Impact & Policy", "Hackathon 2026"
   - Font size: 12px

5. **Blue Horizontal Line** (blue #1565c0, thin ~1-2px)
   - Full width separator

6. **Body Content** (justified Georgia serif, 11px, 16px leading)
   - Paragraphs in justified Georgia serif, black text
   - **Section Headings: BLUE #1565c0, BOLD, NO ASTERISKS**
     - ❌ WRONG: **What we noticed in your application**
     - ✓ CORRECT: What we noticed in your application (rendered as blue bold text, no ** symbols)
   - All text uses "We" voice, never "I"

## CSS/Styling Details:

- **Logo:** Centered, ~0.8" height
- **Small header:** Blue #1565c0, 10-11px, uppercase, letter-spaced
- **Main title:** Blue #1565c0, 16-18px, bold, centered
- **Subtitle:** Blue #1565c0, 12px, centered
- **Dividing line:** Blue #1565c0, thin (~1-2px)
- **Body font:** Georgia serif, 11px, justified
- **Body line height:** 16px leading
- **Headings in body:** Blue #1565c0, bold, NO ** ASTERISKS (render as clean blue bold, not markdown)

## Critical Points:

1. **NO ASTERISKS IN HEADINGS** — headings should be pure blue bold text
2. **Blue line, not black** — horizontal divider is blue
3. **Two-line position header** — main title + subtitle
4. **Justified Georgia throughout body** — matching the professional look
5. **"We" voice always** — never "I"
