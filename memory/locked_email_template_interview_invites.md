# LOCKED EMAIL TEMPLATE — ALL INTERVIEW INVITES

**STATUS:** 🔒 LOCKED FOR ALL FUTURE USE — UPDATED 2026-05-13

**APPLIES TO:** ALL interview stage invites (values, warm bench, case study, GWC, zero-in, final, offer, etc.) for ALL positions.

**RULE:** Every interview invite email MUST follow this template exactly. Design is universal across all stages — only content changes per stage type.

**KEY UPDATE (2026-05-13):** Color palette simplified to 2 core colors. Blue for all text/headers/dividers. Purple for CTA button.

---

## HTML Structure & Design Specification

### PAGE WRAPPER
```
Background: #f3f4f6 (very light grey)
Padding: 60px top/bottom
Font family: Georgia, serif (body fallback)
```

### EMAIL CARD (Main Container)
```
Background: #ffffff (white)
Max width: 620px
Center aligned
Padding: 60px top/bottom, 70px left/right
Border radius: 8px
Box shadow: 0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.04) [subtle shadow only]
```

## Spacing Rules (LOCKED)

- Logo to header label: 24px
- Header label to title: 24px
- Title to subtitle: 10px
- Subtitle to divider: 32px
- Divider to greeting: 50px (before body)
- Greeting to first paragraph: 18px
- Between paragraphs: 18px
- Last paragraph to button: 40px
- Button to button subtitle: 50px
- Button subtitle to footer divider: 60px
- Footer divider to footer: 50px

**Design must feel: AIRY, FORMAL, LIKE A PRINTED LETTER.**

---

## What to AVOID (LOCKED — VIOLATIONS TRIGGER RESEND)

- ❌ Bright blue (use only `#4169E1` and `#4b6cb7`)
- ❌ Reduced spacing (all margins/padding FIXED)
- ❌ Modern UI fonts (Inter, Poppins, system sans-serif for body)
- ❌ Grey body text (pure `#000000` only)
- ❌ Card width other than 620px
- ❌ Padding other than 70px left/right, 60px top/bottom
- ❌ Divider thickness other than 1px
- ❌ Shadow other than `0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`
- ❌ Marketing email tone (keep formal, official, respectful)
- ❌ Excessive decoration (logos and lines only)
- ❌ Compressed text (maintain 1.75 line height)
- ❌ Mixed fonts (Georgia serif + Arial for labels only)
