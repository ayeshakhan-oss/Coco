# Sub-skill: Internal Team Move (New Contract)

Sub-skill of [Skill 07 — Contract Drafting](SKILL.md). Read the master workflow + [TEMPLATE_MAP.md](TEMPLATE_MAP.md) first. Mirrors `Contracts\Promotion\` — Ayesha's staging folder of contract masters from all entities for exactly this case (2026-08-12).

---

## When this applies

An existing employee is moving **from one team to another team** → prepare a **NEW CONTRACT**, not an Addendum. If the situation is ambiguous, ask: *"Is this a promotion/change within the same team, or is the employee moving to a different team?"* (Same team → [addendum-promotion.md](addendum-promotion.md).)

## Procedure

1. Confirm it's a team move (ask if ambiguous) → confirm the **destination entity + engagement type** with Ayesha (never assume they carry over unchanged).
2. Route to the destination entity's sub-skill ([opl-contracts.md](opl-contracts.md) / [owt-contracts.md](owt-contracts.md) / [inc-contracts.md](inc-contracts.md) / [niete-contracts.md](niete-contracts.md)) and prepare the new contract exactly as a fresh hire's — new designation, new team, new JD under Annexure-A, dates per Ayesha.
3. NDA: confirm with Ayesha whether a fresh NDA is required or the existing one stands — never assume.
4. Run the full validation checklist in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) — the leftover-previous-details scan matters doubly here (same person, new document).
5. Internal team-move / new-contract email: ⏳ template pending — flag until shared.
6. Show Ayesha the complete package. No send without explicit approval.
