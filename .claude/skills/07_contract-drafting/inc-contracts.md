## Inc. contract — fill fields (yellow-highlighted)

Current date (top + signature block) · Employee name (top + acceptance) · Effective date — **"Date Month, Year" format** · Name + "Designation" in the parties clause · Compensation: **PKR XYZ/month, "disbursed in dollars at that time of conversion" (all-inclusive package)** · Acceptance: **CNIC/Passport/Company registration (X-Y-Z)**.
**Fixed (never touch):** the "technical services and operation management" WHEREAS wording; Clause I bank-charges allocation (US/Pakistan); Attachment A terms (IP, confidentiality, etc.); signatory **Haroon Yasin, CEO**.

## Required information (ask only what's missing)

Full legal name · CNIC or Passport or Company registration number · designation · effective date · monthly PKR amount · **JD (ask Ayesha every time)** → under **Annexure-A inside the same document** (Ayesha 2026-08-12).

## Procedure

1. Copy master → `output/contracts/<Full Name>/Contract - <Full Name> - Taleemabad Inc.docx`. Never overwrite the master.
2. Fill ONLY yellow fields; clear highlighting on filled runs. Match the "Date Month, Year" format exactly.
3. JD under Annexure-A.
4. Run the contract validation checklist in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md).
5. Package with the Permanent Employee NDA ([ndas.md](ndas.md)).
6. Joining email: ⏳ template pending — flag until shared.
7. Show Ayesha the complete package. No send without explicit approval.
