## OPL Full-Time — fill fields (yellow-highlighted)

Header: Date, CNIC, Name · Body: Mr./Ms. NAME, position, **team**, commencement date · Compensation: Gross Salary PKR XYZ/month (inclusive of Medical & Other Allowance, subject to tax) · Acceptance: NAME + CNIC.
**Fixed (never touch):** probation 3 months; leave policy link; termination clauses; 3-working-day acceptance validity; Orenda Private Limited letterhead (Plot 9, Times Square, Korang Road, I-10 Markaz).

## Required information (ask only what's missing)

Full legal name + salutation (always ask Mr./Ms., never infer) · CNIC · designation + team · commencement date · gross salary PKR/month · **JD (ask Ayesha every time)** → under **Annexure-A inside the same document**.

## Procedure

1. Copy master → `output/contracts/<Full Name>/Contract - <Full Name> - OPL Full-Time.docx`. Never overwrite the master.
2. Fill ONLY yellow fields; clear highlighting on filled runs.
3. JD under Annexure-A.
4. Run the contract validation checklist in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) (leftover-details scan, placeholder scan, diff-vs-master clause check, formatting).
5. Package with the correct NDA ([ndas.md](ndas.md)) — contract + NDA together, always.
6. Joining email: ⏳ template pending from Ayesha — flag "email template missing" until shared.
7. Show Ayesha the complete package. No send without explicit approval.
