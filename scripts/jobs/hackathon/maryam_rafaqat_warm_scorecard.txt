Subject: Where we saw the gap

Dear Maryam,

We're not moving forward. I'm going to be direct about why.

**What I saw**

You engaged with the problems we presented. You attempted to think through them. You completed projects. That shows discipline and follow-through.

But here's what concerned me: When I asked you to explain how something worked, you couldn't. Not because you didn't know - but because you hadn't actually learned it. You'd used a tool. You'd applied it. But you couldn't explain the underlying concept.

That gap - between using a tool and understanding what it does - that's the critical gap.

**Why this matters**

Tools change. Frameworks become obsolete. Languages evolve. If you're building your capability on "I know how to use X tool," then every time the tool changes, you have to start over.

But if you understand the principles underneath - the concepts, the logic, the "why" - then you can learn any new tool. That's the difference between being dependent on specific tools and being actually capable.

**What you need to do**

When you work on a project, don't just follow steps or apply tools. Actually understand what's happening. When you use an AI tool to generate code, read the code. Ask yourself: What is this doing? Why does it work this way? Can I explain this to someone else?

Build fewer projects, but understand each one deeply. That's better than building many projects where you're unclear about how they actually work.

You have the discipline to complete work. Now invest in understanding that work deeply.

That's where your real growth comes from.

Maryam
